# Frozen scope

- Milestone: candidate-3 full-session placement and launch-readiness handoff.
- Decision: define the exact H5 checks and placement contract that must pass before a separate full-session run contract may be released.
- Inputs: accepted candidate-3 host/storage feasibility packet and review, active-path map, readiness inventory, whole-stage-restart evidence, and the active divergence plan only.
- Completion: an immutable, machine-checkable H5-preferred checklist whose successful execution would establish operational placement readiness, without claiming H5 is ready now.
- Exclusions: no H5 contact, voltage read, payload hash, transfer, materialization, service installation, launch, sort, RF/holdout access, or scientific outcome inspection.
- Stop: seal the specification and focused review; do not perform any listed fresh H5 check in this task.
