## Concrete staged expansion

1. **Qualify a multidepth batch before expanding duration.** Begin with154,246,317,445,463,510,549,587using the existing frozen identity/injection protocol and dense local labeled/unknown competitor controls. Reuse154's exact compatible completed controls where justified by hashes; the other quiet passes must not inherit its qualification. Keep80as a separately flagged diagnostic, not a required dependency. Review554/612as alternative deep identities, then the nine unreviewed eligible leads without promoting the known duplicate family. Do not change scoring or acceptance thresholds to reach a target count.
2. **Preflight temporal transfer on two bounded20s blocks:4280–4300 and4400–4420s.** Select these before examining their motion agreement. Check frozen-bank identity support, competing hypotheses, boundary shifts and unselected waveform mixtures. They become development evidence once inspected. If existing identities fail, use independently detected local waveform cohorts as candidate leads in those blocks, with separate adjacent qualification blocks; do not connect them to old identities by shape alone.
3. **Track qualified survivors together over4160–4460s.** Reuse exact compatible central100s artifacts; process only missing20s voltage blocks once for all units. Keep five-second observation bins with the existing minimum10events,80%dominant unique-shift support and no boundary-shift population. Preserve rejected/ambiguous events and gaps. Use a provisional goal of at least6qualified distinct identities spanning at least4depth regions for this pilot; if the data cannot provide it, report the shortfall instead of treating the numerical target as a gate override.
4. **Extend to4460–4760s only after the300s evidence is interpretable.** Freeze matching/measurement choices before this extension and report the new300s separately as temporal transfer. Replace unsupported old identities with explicitly new overlapping cohorts when needed. Never silently merge identities across a gap or extrapolate fixed-support centroids beyond their qualified range.
5. **Grow toward20–30by filling depth/time gaps.** Prioritize roughly700–1200µm and other uncovered bands, rather than harvesting more similar3000–3600µm shapes. Use existing sort labels as seed proposals alongside independently detected cohorts. Discovery, qualification and reporting can run as separate agent tasks; numerical work should share one bounded voltage/event cache and parallelize candidate controls or time blocks under the existing managed-job/resource limits.

This is a staged proposal, not authorization to claim six or twenty qualified cells exist. A useful outcome can be fewer distinctive identities with explicit coverage and additional unlinked tracklets. There is no need to implement a whole new sorter or a continuous-motion prior merely to fill missing lighthouse observations.

## Two failures must guide the next work

Unit80's contextual weighting improved quiet recall47.7%→80.5% and injected unique-shift recovery55.3%→92.7%. Its worst-rival false-target rate remained3.33%, exceeding the frozen1%gate. Several labeled rivals and one unknown family each produced one acceptance in30trials. This is a specificity failure under the recorded test, with coarse small-denominator uncertainty; it is not fixed by running many more easy trials to dilute the rate. First inspect those saved failed events/hypotheses to distinguish confusion, collision/background effects and possible duplicate cohorts. Do not remove rival identities post hoc merely to pass. This bounded inspection can run independently of the multidepth expansion.

Unit154's measurement was revised after the original **median of individual-event centroids** compressed approximate injection shifts, with gain-dependent response slopes0.34–0.63. Those slopes do not describe the newer **centroid of the median waveform**. At gain1, the newer statistic responds−9.09/+6.92µm to requested−10/+10µm interpolations; the clean injected-template centroids themselves change−9.46/+7.24µm, so part of the directional asymmetry is in the approximate interpolation model. The revised real transition change is−5.77µm, rather than the earlier−1.23µm. Use the revised median-waveform statistic as the starting measurement for expansion, with identity, support and conditional uncertainty kept separate. Do not transfer old event-centroid slope corrections to it or claim these injection controls establish physical ground truth. Two later five-second transition bins still lack support.
