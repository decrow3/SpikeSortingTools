# DE common saved-output outcome scorecard

This source extends DC's exact half-open, piecewise-linear motion domains to
manifest-defined W2/W3 saved DARTsort and full-session rescue Kilosort outputs.
It preserves the first-depth field, centered 480-knot baseline, `< -120 um`,
outside-mask `abs < 20 um`, and exhaustive remainder definitions. The
remainder is never called true rest.

All units, noise, good-labelled Kilosort subsets, catalogue cross-tabs,
segment-safe 8/9--29/30-sample ISIs, exact exposures and arm-local rate-rank
associations are retained. Unit IDs are not joined across arms. Kilosort's
full-session training context and absence of DARTsort-style negative noise
labels are explicit limitations. The outer RF holdout remains sealed.
