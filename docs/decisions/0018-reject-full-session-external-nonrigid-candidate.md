# Decision 0018: Reject the tested full-session external-nonrigid candidate

**Status:** accepted, 2026-09-28

The completed Luke0804 imec0 external-nonrigid MEDiCINe 12/9 candidate does not
replace the rescue 12/9 motion-off operational reference.

The identity-bound full-session comparison has only 20 measurable pairs among
586 baseline-eligible interior units (3.41%, below the frozen 50% floor), so its
amplitude-completeness endpoint is infeasible. It also exceeds the prospective
0.01 regression limit for median refractory violations (+0.01375), chance-aware
near-coincident excess (+0.01101), and edge-unit fraction (+0.01161). The
candidate yields 526 curated units and 129 KS-good units versus 710 and 301 in
the reference, with only 72 reciprocal primary correspondences.

Retain the candidate artifacts as negative development evidence. Do not rerun
or promote this exact field/application/sorter combination. The conclusion does
not generalize to every motion estimator or correction operator; a future
candidate must first provide a qualified field, feasible identity/coverage
endpoint, and the same population guardrails.
