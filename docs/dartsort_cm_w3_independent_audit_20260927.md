## Three distinct comparison domains

The packet correctly separates:

1. **Fixed pre-source hard map:** identical 597 immediate-pre-agglomeration
   source units. Gated has 590 components, no-force 597 and all-force 491.
2. **Final survivors/labels on fixed times:** the packet's `fixed` ISI result;
   it is not the pre-source partition metric.
3. **Actual final state:** branch-specific final times, survivors and labels.

All branches retain 571,935 row slots. Actual assigned/noise counts are
565,063/6,872 for all-force, 566,611/5,324 for gated and 566,629/5,306 for

label differences are descriptive only and are not called reassignment.

largest and lower-median changed all-force components independently recompute to
parents 124 (12 source units) and 293 (two). Their raw-scale panels use 182 exact
physical positions and are not outcome-picked best examples.

## Outcome safeguards

Rest ISI 9--29-sample fractions are 0.01078 all-force, 0.00602 gated and 0.00600
no-force; episode fractions are 0.00936, 0.00460 and 0.00459. Gated is therefore
slightly worse than no-force on this count, while both are far below all-force.
Because splitting mechanically lowers short-ISI counts, this is not primary
success evidence.

The 424 split-template rows all use exact common physical positions with
positive radial event support for both units: support ranges 16--36 positions
(median 26), and the alignment sign is recorded. All cosines are finite; median
is 0.945 and 308/424 are at least 0.90. These are broad recorded-footprint
compatibility diagnostics, not identities. The visual panels are legible and
consistent with the deterministic selections.

No old CD IDs are joined to W3. RF and independent identity evidence remain
required before any neuronal-yield or merge-benefit claim.
