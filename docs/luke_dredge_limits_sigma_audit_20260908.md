# DREDGE displacement-limit and detector-sigma audit

The field compared with the17 waveform candidates is the compensated5σ replay over930–1030s. Its settings explicitly use nonrigid Gaussian windows (300µm sigma,200µm center spacing,50µm margin parameter),1µm depth bins,1s time bins,1µm depth smoothing,1s time smoothing,60s temporal horizon,max_disp_um80 and mincorr0.1. Saved window centers span10–3810µm (20 centers), so this is not a search restricted to one small probe region.

`testing/luke_dredge_bounded.py` explicitly masks candidate correlation lags to|lag|≤80µm and reruns the solver with those bounded pairwise shifts. This is a pairwise registration search bound, not an absolute clamp on the final integrated displacement field. A rapid200–400µm displacement cannot be recovered directly by that pairwise search; chained smaller relative shifts could still yield a larger total trajectory. Wrong in-range correlation peaks or rejected pairs mean failure need not appear as output pinned at80µm. In this early run,3.30% of positive-weight off-diagonal pairs within60s select exactly±80µm; they carry1.29% of stored U weight. These fractions do not measure how much movement was missed. Broad spatial windows and1s temporal smoothing are additional possible attenuation mechanisms, not established causes from this audit.

The fresh detector3/4/5/6σ sweep was on4160–4260s, not930–1030s. All arms retained the same±80µm bound and estimation configuration. Recomputing temporal peak-to-peak excursion separately at each of20 depth centers gives median-across-depth amplitudes of8.58,8.97,11.29 and14.88µm at3,4,5 and6σ respectively. Central95% temporal ranges give7.35,7.19,8.49 and12.71µm. Relative to5σ,3σ decreases peak-to-peak range at17/20 depths;4σ at13/20;6σ increases it at14/20. Effects are therefore depth dependent, but lowering sigma generally reduced amplitude and increasing sigma generally increased it in that interval. This is distinct from the previously reported agreement scores and1s-increment statistics.

The cheapest next causal check is to reuse exactly the early5σ peaks/localizations and compare the existing±80µm bound with predeclared wider bounds, holding all other settings fixed. No new spike detection or sorting is needed. This would establish sensitivity to the search limit, not certify waveform identity or physical motion accuracy. Detector-threshold transfer to930–1030s is a separate test; the later sweep cannot establish its direction there.
